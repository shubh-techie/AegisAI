# Invention research candidates

Date: 2026-09-25 | Baseline: AEGISAI-012
Status: **RESEARCH / INVENTION CANDIDATE ONLY**; mechanisms **PLANNED** for investigation.

Working patent research candidate:

> Systems and Methods for Policy-Bounded Closed-Loop Adaptive Security Control Using Behavioral and Operational Risk in Distributed Computing Systems

This working title records a research direction. It does not establish an
invention, a filing, ownership, priority, final claim scope or patentability.

| Candidate | Potential mechanism to investigate | Evidence gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Continuous risk-state evolution across sequential distributed requests | Specify state scope, ordering, update rules and reproducible sequential experiments |
| B | Deterministic policy arbitration between probabilistic behavioral intelligence and enforcement | Specify arbitration and compare inappropriate actions against direct enforcement |
| C | Combined behavioral + operational-state risk evaluation | Specify fusion and conduct matched ablations with independent labels |
| D | Guardrail/stabilization mechanisms preventing unsafe closed-loop security responses | Define unsafe response, scope/duration limits, oscillation, recovery and failure tests |
| E | Explainable risk-state propagation across distributed interactions | Define evidence lineage, trust, causality and explanation assessment |

**Patentability and novelty have NOT been established.** Prior-art review and
patent-counsel review are required before making patentability claims. No candidate
is asserted patentable. The mechanisms are to be evaluated, subject to literature
and prior-art review; overlap with existing systems may narrow or eliminate them.

This task neither performs that review nor produces experimental evidence. See
[Proposed contribution](PROPOSED_CONTRIBUTION.md), [Claims register](CLAIMS_REGISTER.md)
and [Research program](RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md).
