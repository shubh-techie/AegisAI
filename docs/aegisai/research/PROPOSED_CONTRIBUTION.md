# Proposed research contribution

Date: 2026-09-25 | Baseline: AEGISAI-012
Status: **PLANNED RESEARCH**, to be evaluated, subject to literature and prior-art review.

AegisAI does **not** claim invention or novelty of RBAC, ABAC, Zero Trust,
risk-based authorization, behavioral anomaly detection, machine learning,
autonomous incident response, or feedback systems. It makes no priority or
superiority claim for their combination. Defining this architecture is not proof
of an original contribution.

The proposed contribution to investigate is the combined architecture and
experimental evaluation of:

1. Deterministic policy enforcement.
2. Contextual risk assessment.
3. Behavioral risk evidence.
4. Distributed-system operational state.
5. Explainable risk/decision evidence.
6. Policy-bounded AI: probabilistic intelligence informs decisions but does not
   possess unrestricted enforcement authority.
7. Bounded adaptive responses: ALLOW, STEP-UP, LIMIT, DENY.
8. Closed-loop feedback: decision outcomes contribute to subsequent risk-state
   evaluation.
9. Reproducible comparison against deterministic Models A–C.

This is a research hypothesis about a combined design and its evaluation, not an
established result. Model C already provides deterministic scoring and decision
reasons. Protected-resource enforcement, behavioral/operational intelligence,
state evolution and feedback remain planned. Explainable evidence must eventually
be assessed for correctness, fidelity and usefulness; emitting reasons alone
does not demonstrate understandable or effective AI decisions.

A literature and prior-art review must establish overlap, limitations and any
supportable distinction before stronger contribution language is used. Findings
may narrow or invalidate the proposed contribution. See the
[claims register](CLAIMS_REGISTER.md), [conceptual model](CONCEPTUAL_MODEL.md),
and [hypotheses](RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md).
